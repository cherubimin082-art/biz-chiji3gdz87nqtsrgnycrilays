About Us

RAKHI BALA'S HINDI TUTORIAL (LIVE ONLINE CLASS)

Rakhi Bala's Hindi Tutorial is a trusted online Hindi tutor and live learning centre dedicated to teaching Hindi to students of all ages and skill levels. Based in Periakottai near Udumalpet, Tamil Nadu, we offer personal, one-to-one Hindi classes for students across India — including Hindi classes for Tamil speakers, with explanations offered in Tamil wherever it helps students learn Hindi through Tamil more comfortably — via engaging, interactive live sessions led by an experienced and passionate tutor. Our highly rated programme — scoring an impressive 4.9 out of 5 — reflects our unwavering commitment to student success and personalised attention.

We offer flexible class timings on weekdays from 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM, with flexible weekend timings on Saturdays and Sundays, making it easy for school students, working professionals, and homemakers to fit Hindi learning into their busy schedules. Whether you are a complete beginner or looking to improve your reading, writing, or spoken Hindi skills, Rakhi Bala's Hindi Tutorial provides a warm, structured, and results-driven online Hindi course right from your screen.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about learning Hindi online with us

What are online Hindi classes?

Online Hindi classes are live Hindi lessons conducted over video call instead of in person. You join from home on your phone or laptop, and the tutor teaches, corrects, and interacts with you in real time — the same as a physical classroom, just online.

Who can join Rakhi Bala's Hindi classes?

Anyone — school students, college students, working professionals, and homemakers. Classes are grouped by age and current Hindi level, so complete beginners and advanced learners are both welcome.

Are Hindi classes available for beginners?

Yes. Beginner Hindi classes start from the alphabet, pronunciation, and basic vocabulary, and build up gradually to sentence formation and simple conversation.

Do you provide Hindi tuition for school students?

Yes. School Hindi tuition is aligned with school syllabi to help students with homework, exam preparation, and scoring better in Hindi as a subject.

Do you offer spoken Hindi classes for adults?

Yes. Spoken Hindi classes for adults focus on everyday conversation, confidence, and fluency for work, travel, or daily life — not just grammar and writing.

Are one-to-one Hindi classes available?

Yes. Alongside small group batches, we offer one-to-one personal Hindi tutoring, with the pace and content tailored entirely to the individual student.

How can I join the Hindi classes?

Fill out the enquiry form above or message us directly on WhatsApp with your requirements. We'll get back to you with the right batch, timing, and next steps.

Where is Rakhi Bala's Hindi Tutorial located?

We're based in Periakottai, near Udumalpet, Tamil Nadu, India. Since all classes are conducted online, we teach students across Tamil Nadu and the rest of India as well.
